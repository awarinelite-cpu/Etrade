How it works

One command sets the whole alert

/alertBTCbelow60000BUY
the command
Always starts with /alert — the bot only listens for its own commands.
the coin
BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP, or DOGE. More added as people ask.
the direction
above or below — which side of the price you're watching for.
the price
Your number. Checked against the live price every 5 minutes.
the label (optional)
BUY or SELL — tags the alert for your own reference. Purely a label — Signal never places a trade.
What Signal does
  • Checks live prices every 5 minutes
  • Messages you the instant your target is hit
  • Tracks unlimited alert history
  • Lets you tag alerts BUY / SELL for your own notes
What Signal doesn't do
  • Never places a trade
  • Never touches your exchange account
  • Never holds or moves your funds
  • Never gives financial advice — you set every number
